Function: Horn breakout board to provide +24V and digital signal from dashboard to horn
Inputs: +24V input from Low Voltage Bus Board, Digital signal input from Dashboard.

Outputs: +24V horn output (switched on/off).

Key Components: relay or MOSFET for switching, connectors, maybe fuses, etc.

Electrical constraints: voltage levels, current limits, connector type, board size restrictions.

Interfaces: how it connects to the rest of the system (pin headers, Molex connectors, etc.).
